Preparation checklist

Before travelling

  1. Check passport validity.
  2. Confirm visa and official requirements.
  3. Review transport and accommodation details.
  4. Keep copies of important documents.

Useful items

  • Passport and approved travel documents
  • Comfortable walking footwear
  • Personal medicines, if applicable
  • Emergency contact information
